Problem Statement

AI-Powered Environmental Monitoring

A resilient, AI-powered environmental monitoring network that provides early detection, localized intelligence, and actionable alerts for floods, forest fires, pollution events, and other environmental hazards common in India, enabling authorities and communities to shift from reactive disaster response to proactive risk prevention.

Problem Statement ID – 26178 Theme – Disaster Management Category – Hardware
The Problem

India faces many hazards at once

Traditional monitoring depends on centralized infrastructure and continuous connectivity, so by the time a warning goes out, it is often too late.

Delayed Detection

Isolated Data

Reactive Response

Feasibility

Feasibility & Viability

Technical Feasibility

  • Ultra-Light Edge AI — 8-bit TinyML, <180 KB SRAM.
  • Low Latency — <25 ms decision time.
  • Dual Communication - Wi-SUN FAN + GSM/4G fallback.
  • Off-Grid Operation — Local alerts + data storage.

Operational & Power Feasibility

  • Adaptive Sampling — Deep sleep normally; increases sampling when risk rises.
  • Solar Powered — 100W solar panel supports continuous operation.
  • Low Power — ~3.2 mA average consumption.
  • Fault Handling — Watchdog + outlier filtering reduce false triggers.

Economic & Cost Viability

  • Low CapEx — ₹8,000 per node.
  • Affordable Gateway — ₹20,000 VENTUNO Q gateway for 10 nodes.
  • Low OpEx — Intelligent packets reduce data & cloud costs.
  • Scalable — 10 nodes + 1 gateway = ₹1,00,000.
Challenges
Strategy to Overcome
Compute Limits — Complex AI processing is constrained by ESP32-S3’s limited memory and processing power.
TinyML Optimization — Use INT8 models for fast, low-memory inference.
Communication Loss — Severe weather can disrupt cellular and radio connectivity.
Hybrid Communication - Use Wi-SUN + GSM with offline data storage.
Sensor Errors — Drift, dust, and temperature changes can cause false alarms.
Spatial Validation — Cross-check anomalies across nearby nodes.
Power Drain — High-alert monitoring and frequent transmissions can quickly drain batteries.
Adaptive Power — Use sleep modes and event-based sampling.

Research & References

Coimbatore Field Visit – Key Finding

During our visit to the Coimbatore Fire Station, we learned that firefighters mainly depend on district-level forecasts, with limited real-time, location-specific intelligence.

This creates difficulty in identifying which exact area is flooding, landsliding, or becoming hazardous.

Based on their feedback, VISIONX deploys permanent TinyML Edge Nodes to detect local anomalies early and send alerts through Wi-SUN FAN (Field Area Network), enabling a shift from reactive rescue to proactive prevention.
